Domain Index by Type

This section organizes domains and hostnames by their primary function or service.

Select a category below to browse its corresponding entries.

Browse by Category

Advertising & Ad Tech
Domains used for digital advertising, ad delivery, bidding, campaign management, and conversion tracking.

AI & Machine Learning
Domains used by AI assistants, AI model APIs, and AI features within apps and services.

Analytics & Telemetry
Domains used to collect and analyze data on user activity, traffic, crashes, errors, and application performance.

Application & Web Services
Domains supporting applications and websites, including APIs, SDKs, backend services, configuration, feature flags, and search.

Audio, Video & Media
Domains used to deliver and stream audio, video, images, and other media.

Cloud Hosting & CDN
Domains used for cloud hosting, storage, and content delivery, including fonts, code libraries, and static files.

Device, Browser & App Store
Domains used by operating systems, web browsers, app stores, and device services, including updates and downloads.

DNS & Network
Domains supporting DNS, connectivity, routing, time synchronization, and network services.

Help, Docs & Support
Domains used for help centers, documentation, developer portals, community forums, and status pages.

Identity & Authentication
Domains used for identity management, authentication, account verification, and access control.

Location & Geo
Domains providing geolocation, mapping, navigation, and location-based services.

Messaging & Realtime
Domains supporting messaging, chat, voice and video calls, push notifications, and real-time communication.

Payments & Commerce
Domains used for payments, checkout, subscriptions, banking, and online shopping.

Productivity & Workspace
Domains used for email, documents, file storage, contacts, calendars, tasks, and other productivity tools.

Security & Privacy
Domains used for bot protection, fraud prevention, safe browsing, password checks, consent management, and privacy controls.

Social Platforms
Domains associated with social networks, content sharing, and social interactions.

About This Index

  • Categories are organized by their primary function.
  • Most domains are listed under the one category that best represents their primary role. A few that clearly serve two roles, such as social platform media servers, appear in both.
  • This index provides a functional classification of the domains and hostnames documented in this repository.

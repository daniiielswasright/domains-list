About This Index

  • The A–Z index lists every domain alphabetically by its root domain, so related subdomains stay grouped together. For example, "api.stripe.com" is listed under S for Stripe.
  • The type index groups domains by their primary function.
  • The provider pages group domains by the company that owns them.
  • Under each domain is a short note on what it does, and a coloured dot: green is safe to block, amber turns off one feature, red breaks the app or site.
  • Most domains appear in one category. A few that clearly serve two roles, such as social platform media servers, appear in both.

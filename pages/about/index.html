About

I've always been curious about how the internet actually works behind the scenes: which domains do what, why certain websites and services exist, and who our devices are really talking to.

That curiosity led me to start using PCAPdroid, an open-source Android app that shows every connection your apps make. Watching apps quietly reach out to dozens of domains for ads, analytics, tracking, content delivery, and more made me want to understand what each one is for.

This list is the result: every domain I've come across, organized alphabetically and by what it does, so anyone can look up a domain and see what it's used for.

Tools I Use

  • PCAPdroid — captures and shows the network connections each Android app makes, with no root needed.
  • NextDNS — a DNS service that can block domains and log which ones your devices look up.
  • Blokada — an ad and tracker blocker for phones that blocks domains using blocklists.
